Betrayal by friends can feel extremely painful because it breaks trust and emotional safety.
Feeling left out or excluded by a group can make someone feel alone and hurt.
It is okay to take space from people who hurt you, even if they are close friends.
Not having other friends can make decisions about relationships feel more difficult.
It’s normal to feel confused when emotions and practical fears conflict.
Fear of losing people can make it harder to stand up for yourself.
Healthy friendships should include honesty, respect, and support.
It’s okay to prioritize your emotional well-being over maintaining relationships.
Feeling like it’s you against a group can feel overwhelming and isolating.
